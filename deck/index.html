09 · Business model

💸 Free to use. Paid when a pick is worth buying.

Free companion

Reads, routine and daily check-ins cost nothing. Retail partners hold stock, delivery and returns.

Commission on a sale

$45 order × 8% = $3.60 gross. Three orders a year is $10.80 a buyer.

Later: outcome reports

With consent, brands learn what people kept using and what changed. Nobody else has that.

The honest constraint: at $10.80 a buyer a year, acquisition has to stay cheap. That is why the pilot runs on creator links and why the routine, not the scan, is the hook.
